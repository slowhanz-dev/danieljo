When a skincare brand asks for something that feels like morning dew on a tea leaf, the answer is never obvious. A cup and plate conceived as the meeting of water and living green — porcelain as metaphor, executed precisely.

Porcelain as cosmetic packaging

The TR Cream is one of Amore Pacific's most significant products. A limited edition vessel had to earn the right to carry it — not compete with it. The brief asked for something that felt like morning dew on a tea leaf. Which is a brief that could go in many wrong directions.

The answer was a cup and a plate. Two forms that reference the daily ritual of tea — familiar enough to feel right, precise enough to feel considered. The glaze is clear over white. The product is the colour.

01

Form at cosmetic tolerance

Cosmetic packaging requires a different standard than tableware. Surface finish is inspected under controlled lighting. Dimensional tolerance is tighter. The TR Cream vessel was prototyped twelve times before the surface quality met the product's requirements.

The meeting of water and green

The concept was developed from a single image: water touching a living tea leaf. The cup holds the water. The plate holds the cup. Together they describe a moment without illustrating it.

02

Limited edition production

Limited edition runs require the same tooling investment as standard production but with smaller volume to amortise the cost. The TR Cream vessel was produced in a single controlled run, with each piece inspected individually before packaging.
